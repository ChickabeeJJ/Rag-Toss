Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Rag Toss is a free browser game. This page explains, in plain words, what the game does and does not do with information. The short version: it doesn't ask who you are, it has no accounts, no ads and no tracking of its own, and your progress stays in your browser.

What the game stores

To remember your progress, the game saves the following on your own device (in your browser's local storage, or in the CrazyGames save system when you play there):

  • your coins, the throwables and characters you've unlocked, your upgrades and your bag
  • your best scores, ranks, mission progress and lifetime stats (knockouts, headshots and so on)
  • a run you left half-way, so you can continue it
  • your settings: sound, music, screen shake and theme colour

None of this contains your name, email address, location or any other personal detail, and the game never sends it to us. Clearing your browser data, or using Reset progress in the game's settings, deletes it.

What the game does not do

  • It does not collect personal information.
  • It does not have accounts, sign-ups or chat.
  • It does not show ads and does not use advertising or analytics trackers of its own.
  • It does not set its own cookies.
  • It does not sell or share information about you, because it doesn't hold any.

Third parties

CrazyGames. The game is built for the CrazyGames platform and loads the CrazyGames SDK. When you play on crazygames.com, CrazyGames runs the page the game sits in and handles things like your player account (if you have one), cloud saves and any data collected by the site. That is covered by CrazyGames' own privacy policy, not this one.

Hosting. When the game is played from its own web address, it is served by a hosting provider (Vercel), which, like any web host, may keep ordinary server logs such as your IP address and the pages requested, for security and operation. The game itself does not read or use those logs.

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the game ever starts doing something that changes the above (for example adding online leaderboards), this page will be updated first and the date at the top changed.

Contact
